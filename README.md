# CCDV-F Exam Prep (Unofficial)

An unofficial study guide for the **Claude Certified Developer – Foundations** exam (exam code **CCDV-F**).

This repository accompanies the Agentic AI Hub video *CCDV-F Exam Prep: Claude Certified Developer Foundations (2026)*. It is **not** made by Anthropic, it is **not** official training, and it is **not** a promise that you will pass. Always confirm every fact against the official exam guide, Partner Academy FAQ, and Pearson page for your exam date. Anthropic can update the guide.

## Official facts used in this guide (7 December 2026 paper)

Sourced from the official exam guide, Partner Academy FAQ, and Pearson, as they stood when this guide was written. Re-check before you register.

- **70** questions: **60** scored, **10** unscored (the unscored items are not identified)
- Scenario-based multiple choice; each item tells you how many responses to select
- **120** minutes to answer; official FAQ: about **135** minutes of seat time including check-in and a survey
- Closed book; no browser translation tools
- English or Japanese
- Pearson OnVUE or a test centre
- Passing scaled score: **720** on a scale of 100–1,000 (this is not “72% correct”)
- Score report: pass/fail, scaled score, and percent correct by domain (domain percents do not decide the result)
- List price: **$125 USD** before any partner discount
- No formal prerequisites. Recommended: 1–5 years of software engineering and at least 6 months of hands-on work with Claude
- Credential valid **12 months**. On-time renewal is a free, non-proctored Partner Academy assessment. If it lapses, you retake the full exam
- Retakes: 14 / 30 / 90 days; maximum **4** attempts per exam per rolling 12 months; full fee with the same discount; no retake after a pass just to raise the score
- OnVUE: cancel or reschedule up to **1 hour** before. Test centre: **48 hours**. Later changes or a no-show forfeit the fee
- The name on your ID must match your registration. You can update the name once in your Partner Academy exam profile; it then locks. Test-centre name updates need at least 24 hours
- Badges via Credly; age 18+
- The exam is currently open only to **Claude Partner Network** staff who register with a recognised **company email**. Personal emails will not work

Partner-discount figures are stated on the official FAQ (Registered = full price; Select / Preferred / Global Premier = 50% off; Global Premier 100% off through 31 December 2026, then 50%). Confirm the current discount on the FAQ before you pay.

## Official domain weights (exams from 7 December 2026)

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| Applications and Integration | 20% |
| Agents and Workflows | 17% |
| Prompt and Context Engineering | 15% |
| Model Selection and Optimization | 12% |
| Eval, Testing, and Debugging | 10% |
| Tools and MCP | 10% |
| Claude Code | 8% |
| Security and Safety | 8% |

On the older paper (exams through 6 December 2026), Applications and Integration is about **33.1%**, Model Selection about **16.8%**, Agents about **14.7%**, Prompting about **11.0%**, Tools and MCPs about **10.6%**, Security about **8.1%**, Claude Code about **3.1%**, and Evaluation about **2.6%**. The newer paper gives more weight to evaluation, Claude Code, tools, and prompting, and less to applications.

## Suggested study week (newer paper)

Spend the most time on Applications, Agents, and Prompting. Do not skip evaluation, tools, Claude Code, or security — together they are still 36% of the newer paper.

1. Read the official guide that matches your date, end to end.
2. Work one domain a day from the checklists, then do that domain’s questions here.
3. Finish with the mixed mock quiz at the end of `practice-questions.md`.
4. Re-read anything you missed, and confirm numbers on the official FAQ the day before you sit.
